# The Women's Professional Hockey Association
The Women's Professional Hockey Association (WPHA; French:  *association professionnelle de hockey féminin* APHF) is a professional women's ice hockey league in North America, composed of twenty teams (thirteen in the United States, seven in Canada*) Launched in 2013, the WPHA is the only professional women's hockey league in North America. The teams play a regular season of 32 games to earn one of eight places in the playoffs. The winner of the playoffs is awarded the Walter Cup

\*this does not account for the planned relocation of the Detroit Rush to Winnipeg

The WPHA was founded by Lillian Walter and the Beauville Group after the folding of the NWHF. The first draft took place in August of 2013 and the inaugural season began in November of 2013. The league uses a 3-2-1-0 points system and utilizes a gold plan when drafting players
# League Structure
### Conferences
The conference system was established in 2018, with the expansion of the San Francisco Pride, Dallas Blue Bonnets, Halifax Tide, and Las Vegas Roadrunners. Ever since it's inception critics have pointed out the travel burdens placed on the western conference teams, especially the Detroit Rush and Denver Peak, playing teams primarily within their conference.

Teams are guaranteed at least one cross-conference matchup with each other team in the league, although they are not guaranteed whether they are home and away. They are guaranteed a home game and an away game against all teams in their conference.
### Divisions
The current iterations of the divisions were established in the 2025-26 Season with the expansion of the Calgary Comets and the Silver Spring Songbirds. The Mountains Division is the most frequent target of criticism as the majority of the teams reside outside of the mountains and the Denver Peak do not play in it. 

Teams are guaranteed two home games and 2 away games against the members of their division, allowing for the formation of rivalries.
